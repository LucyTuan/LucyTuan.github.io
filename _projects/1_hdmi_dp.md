Perception-based humanoid control that lifts 2D contact tracks into 3D without motion-capture supervision.

- Built a perception pipeline using **CoTracker** and camera geometry to lift 2D contact tracks into 3D for **Isaac Sim**.
- Achieved **0.27 px** 2D and **0.38 cm** 3D contact tracking error without any mocap supervision.
