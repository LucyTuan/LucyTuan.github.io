Solving inverse kinematics with a conditional DDPM instead of a regression network.

- Proposed and implemented conditional-DDPM inverse kinematics.
- Engineered the training and inference pipeline for stable, constraint-aware IK generation.
- Outperformed **IKNet by 14%** and an MLP baseline by **84%** in accuracy.
